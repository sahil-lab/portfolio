# Brand Marks

GitHub and LinkedIn marks are sourced from Simple Icons 11.15.0 and identify
links to the portfolio owner's profiles. They do not imply sponsorship.

- https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/simple-icons@11.15.0/icons/github.svg
- https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/simple-icons@11.15.0/icons/linkedin.svg
- Package license: https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/simple-icons@11.15.0/LICENSE.md

The source outlines are also converted into local numeric contours for the
Three.js architectural footprints. No font or network request is needed to
construct the landmarks at runtime.
