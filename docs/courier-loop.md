# Optional courier round and living encounters

The courier is an original procedural 3D interpretation of the requested pet: periwinkle painted body, scalloped tuft, navy face panel, separate white eyes and smile, rounded arms and feet, and a four-slot tray. `app/courier.ts` exposes named parts for breathing, blinking, looking, walking, greeting, curiosity, pickup, and delivery animation.

`app/delivery-state.ts` owns the optional round. At the Packet Press, E starts a three-second charge with lever/chamber/light animation. Each subsequent E collects one capsule from the four-slot output tray. The rendered courier cargo and press stock use that exact inventory state. Empty stock cannot be collected and repeated deliveries cannot consume another capsule or duplicate the completion reward. All four successful deliveries trigger the completion banner and a celebration voice pattern. Another round requires the explicit action at the Packet Press; it changes delivery fields only, retaining discoveries and leaving portfolio demonstration state alone. Reloading the page starts a fresh session; there is no persistent save system in this slice.
